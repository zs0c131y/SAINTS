About the conference

Discovery to Scalable Solutions

SAINTS is the International Conference on Scientific Advances in Natural & Technological Sciences, hosted by the School of Sciences at CHRIST (Deemed to be University), Bangalore. The 2027 edition takes place on 6–8 January 2027.

The programme has plenary lectures, parallel technical sessions, poster presentations and workshops, organised in four tracks.
